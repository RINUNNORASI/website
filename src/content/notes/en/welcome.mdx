This is example content. Replace it with your research, case studies or project reflections.

## Establish the question and constraints

Record site conditions, user needs and project limits. Support the problem statement with images, data or observations.

## Turn analysis into decisions

Explain which evidence informed each choice. Keep the alternatives and trade-offs visible.

## Present and evaluate the proposal

Use plans, sections, models and scenarios to explain the work. Describe the evaluation, remaining questions and next iteration.
