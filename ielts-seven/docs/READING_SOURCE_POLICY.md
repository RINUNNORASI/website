# Reading passage availability

Checked against publisher pages on 2026-10-16.

## Full passages in the reading workspace

For reliable in-page reading, a course needs a bundled, verified passage and a recorded permission basis. A remotely embedded website is dependent on the source site's availability, framing policy and the reader's network. It cannot provide the same availability guarantee as a locally served passage.

New daily assignments should therefore use courses with both `article.paragraphs` and a supported `article.license.permission` (`licensed` or `public-domain`). Keep attribution and source links in the passage footer. If no unused eligible course remains, explain that the full-passage catalogue is exhausted and offer an existing full-passage course or the archive; do not silently assign a link-only source.

Existing assignments, saved answers and completion records belong to the original course. Preserve them when adding a full-passage selection action. Choosing another course for the current view must not reinterpret the original answers against another article.
